The repeated “why” across targeting failures is the absence of an invariant connecting the mathematical objective to the next stage. A short lattice vector is not evidence of a useful final target unless the transformation preserves the relevant constraints. The new projective construction checks that preservation exactly, while explicitly leaving simultaneous primality unresolved.

## What the new work establishes

The new paper derives a rank-two affine dimension limit, exact projective local counts, a CRT lattice basis, weighted basis preservation, and a finite-product conditioning cancellation. It computes a roughly 3.54944 average local-factor gain for variable differences relative to fixed 23# at comparable sizes. This is not enough to support a practical AP-28 discovery claim.

The portable reference sieve is exact for each specified finite start interval and positive difference, with all terms below 2^64. A five-million-start AP-9 demonstration found eleven progressions, independently checked term by term with SymPy. A ten-million-start AP-28 smoke test found none; its modeled expectation is negligible. Neither result validates the projective proposal's discovery rate.
